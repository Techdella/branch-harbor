For a sports academy or club, a few weeks of registration decide the whole season. Families compare options quickly, popular groups fill in hours, and every mistake turns into emails and refunds. This guide sets out a season plan that works across several venues.

## 8 weeks before registration opens

1. **Define the programmes**: age bands, levels, days, times, venue, coach-to-participant ratio and maximum group size.
2. **Set prices per venue and currency**, including sibling discounts, early registration prices and any instalment options. Write down the refund policy.
3. **Decide what you need to collect**: participant details, guardian contacts, medical and allergy notes, photo consent, emergency contacts. Collect only what you need and decide who can see it.
4. **Check safeguarding requirements** for coaches and volunteers in each country and region you operate in, and record that checks are in place.

## 4 weeks before

1. **Publish the programme pages**, one per venue, with schedules, prices and what to bring.
2. **Prepare messages**: registration confirmation, waitlist confirmation, payment reminder, first-session information.
3. **Test the registration form** on a phone, as a guardian registering two children.
4. **Brief coaches** on group lists, attendance and how to report incidents.

## Registration week

- **Open at a published time** and say so in advance. Families plan around it.
- **Use waitlists** for full groups, with clear positions and automatic offers when places free up.
- **Use approval where needed**. For trial-based or level-based groups, mark the registration as "pending review" and say so in the confirmation, so families know payment and selection are separate steps.
- **Watch capacity daily** and open extra groups where demand justifies it and coaches are available.

## Group assignment

Assign participants to groups by age, level and venue. Keep siblings' schedules compatible where possible. Before announcing groups:

- Check every group meets its ratio.
- Check medical notes are visible to the coaches who need them, and only to them.
- Send each family their group, venue, time and first-session details in one message.

## During the season

- **Take attendance at every session**, on a phone or tablet, and follow up on repeated absences.
- **Communicate changes fast**: weather cancellations and venue changes should reach families within minutes, by email and SMS where they've agreed to it.
- **Track payments** and send reminders for instalments.

## After the season

- Report participation, attendance and retention per venue.
- Survey families with two or three short questions.
- Invite families to register early for the next season.
